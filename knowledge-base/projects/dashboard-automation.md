# Dashboard Automation — Bad Debt, Abuse Metrics, WBR

**Where:** Amazon, Business Intelligence & Data Engineer
**Stack:** Tableau, Redshift, Excel

Three related dashboard/reporting automation efforts, all following the same pattern: replacing
manual, Excel-based, or ad-hoc reporting with self-serve Tableau dashboards.

## Bad Debt Business Level Report
**Problem:** The Bad Debt Business Level Report was maintained manually in Excel every month.
**Approach:** Vivek migrated the Excel-based workflow to Tableau, building ETL pipelines in
Redshift end-to-end.
**Outcome:** Eliminated 8–10 hours of manual work every month (**100+ hours annually**), and
improved consumption by Finance Analysts (a live, always-current dashboard instead of a static
monthly file).

## Abuse Metrics Deep-Dive Dashboards
**Problem:** Business Analysts and Program Managers were submitting frequent ad-hoc requests
for abuse-metrics cuts of the data.
**Approach:** Vivek worked closely with BAs and PMs to understand the underlying data patterns
and built Tableau deep-dive dashboards that let them self-serve.
**Outcome:** Drove adoption of the dashboards and reduced the volume of ad-hoc requests.

## Non-Financials WBR (Weekly Business Review)
**Problem:** Search Marketing (Free + Paid) needed a recurring, reliable Non-Financials WBR.
**Approach:** Vivek owned the generation of this report end-to-end, collaborating with Program
Managers on process improvements and adding new metrics as the business's reporting needs
evolved.
**Outcome:** Introduced **8 new performance metrics** that improved strategic decision-making —
a durable, owned reporting process rather than a one-off report, with Vivek as the continuing
point of contact for its evolution.
