# Keyword Ranking SOP Tool / Google Algorithm Impact Tool (R Shiny)

**Where:** Amazon, Business Intelligence & Data Engineer
**Stack:** R Shiny

## Problem
Whenever Google rolled out a search algorithm update, SEO Marketing Managers needed a fast way
to understand how their keyword rankings had been impacted — without a self-serve tool, this
meant repeated ad-hoc analysis requests every time an algorithm update landed.

## Approach
Vivek developed a Keyword Ranking SOP Tool in R Shiny that let SEO Marketing Managers deep-dive
into ranking movements themselves, directly attributable to specific Google algorithm updates —
turning a recurring ad-hoc request into a self-serve, repeatable workflow (a "Standard Operating
Procedure" tool, hence "SOP").

## Outcome
Enabled **20+ SEO Marketing Managers** to make data-driven search strategy decisions on their
own, reducing deep-dive analysis time from **4 hours to 15 minutes** per keyword investigation —
without waiting on a one-off analysis each time.
