# Benchmark Conversion Rate Model (Keyword Conversion)

**Where:** Amazon, Business Intelligence & Data Engineer
**Stack:** AWS Redshift, R, statistical modelling — partnered with the Automated Marketing Data
Science team

## Problem
Category teams needed a way to know, ahead of time, which search keywords were likely to
under- or over-perform on conversion — without that, opportunity and risk on keyword-level
performance was only visible after the fact.

## Approach
Vivek partnered with the Automated Marketing Data Science team to architect the data pipelines
supporting predictive modelling for Benchmark Conversion Rates — designing the ETL processes
that prepared and transformed data for ML model training. He owned the data engineering side of
the partnership, feeding the model with the right underlying data and translating its output
into something category teams could act on.

## Outcome
Identified **500+ high-opportunity and high-risk keywords quarterly** for category teams to
prioritize, resulting in a **20% improvement in Top 3 SERP rankings** — turning keyword
conversion from a reactive, after-the-fact metric into a forward-looking, actionable one at real
operating scale.
