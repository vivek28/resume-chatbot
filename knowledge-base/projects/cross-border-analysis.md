# Cross-Border Encroachment Analysis

**Where:** Amazon, Business Intelligence & Data Engineer
**Stack:** AWS Redshift, S3, Athena, Glue, Tableau

## Problem
SEO Managers at Amazon needed visibility into "cross-border encroachment" — traffic leaking
into their marketplace from other Amazon domains (e.g. a customer landing on the wrong
country's storefront instead of their local one). Without a clear, quantified view of this,
managers couldn't tell how big the problem was or where to focus corrective action.

## Approach
Vivek architected the Cross-Border Analysis framework and data warehouse from the ground up,
building scalable ETL pipelines that synthesized consumer and market data across **5+
marketplaces** to quantify the magnitude of cross-domain visits into each one, giving SEO
Managers a concrete, data-driven basis for understanding the scale of encroachment.

## Tech Stack
AWS Redshift for the underlying data warehouse, Tableau for the reporting layer, built as part
of his broader Search Marketing / SEO BI work at Amazon.

## Outcome
Identified **$2M+ in potential revenue recovery opportunities** across the 5+ marketplaces
covered — gave SEO Managers a clear, quantified view of cross-border traffic leakage they could
act on directly, on a problem that previously had no dedicated visibility.
