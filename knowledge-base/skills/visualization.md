# Visualization & Reporting

## Tableau
Vivek's primary dashboarding tool at Amazon, used for advanced dashboards and deep-dive
reporting. Examples: Bad Debt Business Level Report (automated from Excel), Abuse Metrics
deep-dive dashboards, Crawl Intelligence Reports, and the Non-Financials WBR for Search
Marketing. See `projects/dashboard-automation.md` and `projects/crawl-intelligence.md`.

## R Shiny
Used to build the Keyword Ranking SOP Tool — a self-serve tool used by 20+ SEO Marketing
Managers to deep-dive into keyword ranking impact after Google algorithm updates, cutting
analysis time from 4 hours to 15 minutes. See `projects/google-algo-tool.md`. Also used for the
Ring IoT self-service analytics tool, letting battery engineers analyze telemetry at multiple
granularities themselves. See `projects/ring-iot-battery-analytics.md`.

## Philosophy
A consistent pattern across his visualization work: replacing static, manual, or ad-hoc
reporting with self-serve dashboards that stakeholders (Finance Analysts, BAs, PMs, SEO
Managers) can use directly — reducing both his own repeated manual effort and the volume of
ad-hoc requests coming to the BI team. Also experienced with interactive HTML reports and
executive summaries.
